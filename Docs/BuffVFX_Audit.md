# Buff presentation audit: all 35 buffs

Date: 2026-11-09. Scope: supplied Prompt 03 foundation and packages 04–08. All 35 mechanics already exist in Practice and the online rules-v3 engine. **35/35 presentation recipes are implemented**: six combat, six mobility, four environment, ten transformation/deployment and nine tactical buffs. Implemented means production hooks, shared rendering and automated checks exist; the unrun live/build/skin checks below remain explicit.

## Project evidence and integration

- Unity 6000.4.7f1, URP 17.4, existing `Assets/Config/New Universal Render Pipeline Asset.asset`. No new package or tween/VFX Graph dependency was added to the game.
- Existing low-poly chess models in `Assets/Skins/Tazji'sLowPoly/Chess_Set`, existing board coordinates, piece movement/skins, Schoolbell font, draft cards and ability HUD are reused. New effects use muted ink, wood, dark metal, brass and restrained teal/gold/violet accents. No Vietnamese UI text was introduced.
- Gameplay sources: `Assets/Scripts/Chess/Gameplay/ARAM/AramBuffRuntime*.cs`, `Assets/Scripts/Chess/Core/ChessGame.Aram.cs`, Domain `AramRules` and `AramBalanceRules`; backend `Online/Rules/Chaos35Engine*.cs`. Existing gameplay is the reference, including rule details that differ from a buff's casual name.
- `AramBuffRuntime.Visuals.cs` projects actual Practice flags and receives accepted outcomes across all 35 buffs and actual board hazard projections. It never runs damage, random rolls, turn changes or capture logic.
- `OnlineAramPresenter.Visuals.cs` feeds `BuffVfxOnlineAdapter` after `ChessGame.ApplyDotNetState` updates the real pieces. The same recipes render Practice and online matches. No extra network transport is needed.
- Existing presentation preferences control reduced motion, effect detail, flashes, shake and animation speed. Effects use unscaled time, obey pause, can skip/fast-forward and never wait for animations before accepting the next authoritative state.
- Implemented buff rings are replaced by distinct persistent glyphs, except temporary setup picking. Authored board props replace legacy marker cubes when the explicit projection is available. Gameplay UI is preserved; older servers retain their legacy marker fallback.

## Presentation contract and lifecycle

`Assets/Resources/BuffVfx/registry.json` validates all 35 actual enum IDs, keys and group ownership. All IDs 0–34 have `implemented: true`. Recipes declare glyph, accent, duration and optional authored prop. The orchestrator does not know about chess boards, rules, RPCs or captures.

Backend adds presentation-only fields to `ChaosState`:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `visualSequence` | Monotonic counter for confirmed presentation events within a match |
| `visualCues` | Durable tail capped at 24; sequence, buff ID, Resolve phase, actor ID, team, from/to squares, outcome, optional Amount, owner-only flag, exact actor changes (ID/team/from/to/origin kind/result kind), actual blast cells / resolved canonical rifle ray |
| `visualStatuses` | Snapshot-only projection of real effects/expiry/risk; excluded from BSON persistence; teleport/swap readiness and escape/rifle resources removed for other viewers |
| `visualHud` | Snapshot-only owner objective/economy projection: exact quest mask, uses, points and owner-turn/combined-move counters; BSON ignored |
| `legalMoveTeam` | Snapshot-only owner of the published legal-move list; lists are removed for other viewers and spectators |
| `visualBoard` | Snapshot-only sanitized cells: Mine, Crate, Warning, Barrier; excluded from BSON persistence; renderer never infers traps from raw gameplay arrays |

Projection runs **before** Doppelganger redaction, using actual trait flags/real buff ownership. A fake displayed Bomber buff does not produce a bomb glyph. No random seed, legal target preview or future betrayal result is sent. Event-envelope projection applies the same privacy policy. Read-only legal queries/snapshots and rejected moves cannot append cues to the original match.

The client maps canonical board coordinates (`a1 = 0,0,0`, +X files, +Z ranks, one tile = one unit) through the real board basis and scale. Events accept finite coordinates, valid squares, at most nine combat/traversal cells, 32 collapse cells or 64 recorded actor changes, known recipes and an explicit viewer authorization flag. No renderer determines blast victims, hit tests, ownership or risk.

First load/reconnect restores current statuses and board props and advances the sequence watermark without replaying historical shots/explosions. Repeated/stale snapshots are ignored. Completed matches clear presentation. This is live snapshot recovery, not a replay-scrubbing implementation; the project currently has no scrub UI connected to these adapters. A future seek should rebuild statuses and reset presentation history at its seek boundary.

Practice uses a per-match visual sequence, actual trait collections and expiry clocks. Status refresh is at most 10 Hz. Persistent icons follow live actors, stack in three columns, billboard toward the gameplay camera and display actual remaining combined plies/owner turns or confirmed percent. Removed statuses dissolve in 0.18 seconds. Team frame shape is a circle for White and diamond for Black; transient caster stamps also preserve ownership independently of effect hue.

All sequences have a recorded world-position fallback when an actor disappears. Cannon uses a faint render-only copy of the real Rook mesh at the **recorded source**, if the authoritative movement has already placed the Rook elsewhere. It has no collider, game component or board occupancy. The real Rook stays at the committed destination. Sniper/King mounts follow the stationary actor; no changes to piece skins/material assets are made.

## Authored assets and visual scope

`Assets/Resources/BuffVfx/Models/{Sniper,KingRifle,Cannon}.obj` are original, imported low-poly models. `tools/build-buff-props.py` reproducibly authors their geometry, with grouped metal/wood/brass/glass parts. These are actual meshes: stocks, receivers, barrels, scope/glass, trigger/guard, sights, bolts, cannon breech/trunnions, collars and support mounts. They replace the old King rifle cube. Bishop has no arm rig; the saddle and two support brackets form a procedural draw/aim mount.

`BoardInk.shader` supplies transparent glyph/line/particle ink; `PropInk.shader` supplies lightweight URP stylized lighting. Both are loaded directly as resources, so they do not depend on a runtime-only `Shader.Find` result. Glyphs and shard meshes are procedural native assets. New prop art is a first implemented low-poly art pass, visually inspected with the existing six chess models, not a claim that every game skin/lighting/camera combination has final art approval. Strategy models `CrownHalo`, `Parachute` and `CreditCard` are likewise original geometry generated by the same tool; no external art or package was downloaded.

Each sequence has draw/anticipation, resolved trajectory/short impact and expiry/retract. Bomber's warning/wave is drawn only on server-provided or locally resolved valid cells; central flash honors flash preferences and never strobes. Native particles use a bounded deterministic cosmetic pattern without consuming Unity's gameplay random stream. No new audio source or music layering is introduced.

## Budgets and observed verification

- Thirty-five prewarmed transient instances; maximum two live instances of each recipe and twelve active total (at most 70 allocated across all per-recipe pools). Excess bursts drop cosmetic events without blocking game state. Event deduplication retains 256 IDs.
- Maximum 64 allocated persistent glyph objects, shared across active/retiring/pool lists. No object/material creation per animation frame. A new actor mesh shape may allocate up to 16 pooled source-echo mesh holders on its first cannon presentation; geometry and material references are shared.
- Board props use per-kind pools with a hard 192-object allocation budget. Current maximum cell projections stay below that cap. No effect lights, gameplay components or enabled colliders are added.
- Five owned shared visual materials per match; at most four grouped mesh renderers per prop. Strategy batches render at most 32 exact cells and eight mesh echoes; result deployments get priority when a removal+spawn batch exceeds 32. Preview pools cap at 64 destination diamonds and 32 ghosts, each with at most 16 mesh holders. Native particle cap is 32 per instance, with normal Bomber bursts of 24 and other impacts of 9, fewer on low detail. Reduced motion suppresses debris and flashes; no dynamic effect lights.
- Scene owner destruction returns effects, unsubscribes render callbacks and disposes owned materials/meshes. Pause cancels a pending camera pulse; the additive pulse is restored after rendering so camera input/raycasts retain the base pose.
- Sequential 60-event reuse, twelve-way overlap, duplicate/unauthorized/invalid input, status stacking/expiry, inactive/destroyed actor, skip/fast-forward, pause, reduced motion, White/Black edge cases, snapshot ordering/reconnect and scene destruction have executable coverage below. These are allocation/cap assertions, **not measured FPS/GPU timing**.
